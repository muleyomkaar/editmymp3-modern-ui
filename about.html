About

EditMyMP3 is a lightweight collection of browser-based MP3 tools designed for quick edits on phones and computers. The editor combines manual controls with an optional AI planner that turns plain-language instructions into edit settings.

What it does

You can trim MP3 files, change volume and speed, add fades, normalize or reverse audio, merge several MP3 files, and convert MP3 to WAV.

How audio is handled

The audio editing and conversion tools are designed to process files locally in the browser. The AI planner receives the text instruction and basic duration information needed to create a plan; it does not need the MP3 file for those commands.
